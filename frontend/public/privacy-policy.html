Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 28, 2026

Welcome to GreenGo India. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy describes how we collect, use, and process your data.

1. Data We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and date of birth when you register.
  • Location Data: Approximate and precise location data to facilitate hot food delivery.
  • Transaction History: Purchase history to show your previous orders and bills.
  • Device Identifiers: Unique ID for push notification updates.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely for app functionality, secure authentication (OTP, Google Login), order processing, and account management. Your data is encrypted in transit using secure HTTPS connections.

3. Account & Data Deletion Policy

We provide full control to our users over their personal data. You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time.

How to request deletion:

Open the app, go to your Profile Settings -> Privacy & Security Center and click on "Request Account Deletion". Your account will enter a 7-day recovery period, after which all personal profile details, location histories, and login sessions will be permanently deleted from our servers.

Alternatively, you can email our support team at princerajmne@gmail.com with your registered phone number requesting account deletion.

4. Data Retention

Account data is deleted within 7 days of deletion request. Inactive accounts with no activity for 30 consecutive days are automatically queued for deletion. Purchase invoices are kept only for tax/revenue compliance.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions, contact us at princerajmne@gmail.com or visit green-go.in.
